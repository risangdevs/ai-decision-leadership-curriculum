## Assumption challenge

For the highest-priority assumption:

- Why do we believe it?
- What evidence supports it?
- What evidence would weaken it?
- What is the cheapest ethical test that could change our mind?
- What will we do if it is wrong?

# Phase 1 readiness check

Do not finalize the workbook until you can defend each answer orally:

- Why do you believe this?
- What evidence supports it?
- Could AI be wrong?
- What assumptions exist?
- Who owns this decision?
- What data would change your mind?

Record any answer that changes during the oral examination in the revision record.
