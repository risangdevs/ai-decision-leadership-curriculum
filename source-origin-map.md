## How to maintain this map

When adding or replacing an external source:

1. Add the source to the relevant curriculum chapter or source guide.
2. Add one record here for every distinct external URL.
3. Name the organization responsible for the content, not merely the website host. If those differ, record both.
4. Classify the source so readers can distinguish law, regulation, standards, government guidance, research, vendor guidance, practitioner material, and community resources.
5. Add every curriculum file key in which the URL appears.
6. For operational use, record an access date and verify version, status, scope, and applicability in the project evidence register.

## Interpretation cautions

- **Origin is not independence.** Vendor guidance may be useful but should not be treated as independent proof of product performance or return on investment.
- **A landing page is not always the source text.** Prefer official publications, legal text, or full standards when making a material claim.
- **Official does not mean universally applicable.** Laws, regulator guidance, and government frameworks must be mapped to jurisdiction, sector, role, and use case.
- **Repository hosting is not authorship.** For academic work, cite the authors and publication venue even when the link points to arXiv or an institutional copy.
- **Source status changes.** Recheck living frameworks, web guidance, product documentation, and legal implementation material before reuse.
